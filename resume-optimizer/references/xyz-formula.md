# Google XYZ formula

> **Accomplished [X], as measured by [Y], by doing [Z].**

- **X - the outcome.** What changed for the business, users or team, not the task.
- **Y - the measure.** A number that proves it: %, $, time, volume, users, rank, or before -> after.
- **Z - the method.** What *you* did: the skills, tools and decisions. This is where JD keywords belong.

The order can change so the bullet reads well. All three parts must be present.

## Before -> after

| Weak (duty) | XYZ |
|---|---|
| Responsible for the checkout API. | Cut checkout API p95 latency by [X%] (from [A] ms to [B] ms) by adding Redis caching and rewriting N+1 queries in PostgreSQL. |
| Worked on marketing campaigns. | Grew qualified leads [X%] quarter over quarter by running [N] A/B-tested email campaigns in HubSpot, segmented by product usage. |
| Helped onboard new hires. | Reduced new-engineer ramp-up from [A] to [B] weeks by writing a 30-day onboarding plan and a local-dev setup script used by [N] hires. |
| Managed social media. | Increased Instagram engagement rate from [A%] to [B%] in [N] months by shifting to a short-form video calendar based on weekly analytics reviews. |
| Did data analysis for sales. | Recovered [$X] in at-risk renewals by building a churn-risk dashboard in SQL + Tableau that the sales team reviewed weekly. |

## When there is no number

Use a credible proxy instead of inventing one:
- **Scale:** "across 4 teams", "for 12k monthly users", "a 40-page spec"
- **Frequency:** "weekly", "for every release"
- **Adoption:** "adopted as the team standard", "used by all 3 regional offices"
- **Recognition:** "promoted after 10 months", "selected to present at the all-hands"
- **Before/after state:** "replaced a manual 2-day process with an automated report"

## Verb bank

Built, Launched, Cut, Grew, Automated, Led, Designed, Shipped, Scaled, Migrated, Negotiated, Reduced, Increased, Drove, Owned, Rebuilt, Standardised, Mentored, Analysed, Forecast, Streamlined, Secured, Delivered.

Avoid: responsible for, helped, assisted, worked on, involved in, participated in, various, etc.
